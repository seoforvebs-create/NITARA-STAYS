Our Story

Born from the Mountain

"We didn't build a hotel. We restored a way of living — and invited the world to share it."

Nitara Stays began as a personal obsession. Its founders — architects, travellers and mountain devotees — believed that the greatest luxury was not thread count or pool temperature, but the quality of silence. The depth of a view. The warmth of food grown in soil you can see from your table.

In Mukteshwar — a ridgeline village at 2,286 metres in the Kumaon Himalaya — they found their answer. A place where Nanda Devi rises above the treeline, where winter air carries the scent of pine and where villagers still know the name of every orchid that grows on the hillside.

Nitara is not a resort that happens to be in the mountains. It is a mountain retreat that happens to have rooms.

What We Believe

The Principles That Guide Us

01

The Land First

Every decision at Nitara begins with a question: does this honour the land? Our buildings follow the ridge. Our gardens follow the season. Our menus follow the soil.

02

Quiet Over Loud

We have no lobby DJ. No Instagram wall. No programmed experience. Instead: birdsong, fire crackle, the sound of snow landing on leaves. The things that matter most are usually the quietest.

03

Deep Localness

Our staff are from Mukteshwar. Our food is from farms within 10 kilometres. Our crafts are from Kumaoni artisans. When you support Nitara, you support a mountain community.

04

Unhurried Time

We gently discourage tight itineraries. Some of our guests' most profound moments happen between scheduled experiences — at dawn, over a second cup of chai, watching clouds fill the valley.

05

Regenerative Luxury

Solar energy. Rainwater harvesting. Composting kitchens. Zero single-use plastics. Our goal is that the land is better for our presence — not merely unchanged by it.

06

Genuine Hospitality

Not performative warmth but the real Kumaoni kind — where you are treated as a guest in someone's home, because that is precisely what this is. A home. Yours for a few days.

Our Journey

From Ridge to Retreat

2018

The Founding Vision

Arjun Mehta first visits Mukteshwar and identifies the ridge at Dhanachuli as extraordinary. The vision for Nitara is born on a misty November morning.

2020

Construction Begins

Using local stone and reclaimed Kumaoni timber, the first property — The Dhanachuli House — begins construction with all-local craftsmen.

2021

First Guests Arrive

Nitara opens quietly, by invitation only. The first guests — a couple from Delhi — stay for 10 days and return the following month. The word begins to spread.

2023

The Collection Grows

Five new properties added across Mukteshwar and Dhanachuli — each hand-selected, each unique in character and view. The Summit Suite wins national recognition.

2024

10,000 Guests Milestone

Nitara welcomes its 10,000th guest. Nine retreats, zero compromise on quality, and a community of mountain lovers spanning 48 countries.

2025

The Future Begins

New retreats in the upper Himalaya planned. The Nitara Foundation launches to support Kumaoni artisans and forest conservation programmes.
