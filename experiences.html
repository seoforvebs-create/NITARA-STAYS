What Awaits You

Six Realms of Himalayan Living

Each experience at Nitara is a doorway — into the forest, into the culture, into yourself. Select freely, or let our concierge curate your perfect days.

Adventure & Trek

Forest Trek & Himalayan Panorama

Walk through oak, rhododendron and pine forests to a ridge offering a 180° view of the Himalayan range — Nanda Devi, Trishul, Panchachuli and Kedarnath visible in perfect clarity. Best October to February.

4–5 Hours 2–8 Guests

₹3,500/person

Wellness & Spa

Himalayan Herbal Wellness Ritual

A 2-hour curated wellness journey using local herbs, mountain minerals and Kumaoni bodywork techniques. Begin with a steam infused with pine needles and end in deep mountain stillness.

2 Hours 1–2 Guests

₹5,000/person

Culinary Journey

Kumaoni Table — Farm to Soul

An immersive culinary journey beginning in our organic garden — harvest your meal, learn the art of Kumaoni bhaang ki chutney, bhatt ki dal and bal mithai from a local home cook. Finish with a terrace dinner under the peaks.

3 Hours 2–6 Guests

₹4,500/person

Cultural Experience

Sacred Circuit — Jageshwar & Kasar Devi

A full-day guided journey to two of India's most spiritually charged sites. Jageshwar Dham — where Shivling puja originated — and Kasar Devi, the meditation site of Swami Vivekananda, known for its extraordinary magnetic field.

Full Day 2–8 Guests

₹6,000/person

Star Observatory

Mukteshwar Night Sky Observatory

At 2,286 metres with minimal light pollution, Mukteshwar offers one of India's finest night skies. A professional guide leads a 2-hour session with high-powered telescopes — planets, star clusters, nebulae and the Milky Way in full splendour.

2 Hours Any group

₹3,000/person

Romantic Escape

Peaks & Petals — The Romantic Retreat

An evening crafted for two — private terrace set with candles and Himalayan wild flowers, a seven-course locally-sourced dinner with Kumaoni wine, followed by a stargazing session with telescopes and warm kahwa under the Milky Way.

3–4 Hours 2 Guests

₹7,500/couple
