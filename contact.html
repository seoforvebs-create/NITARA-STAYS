Getting Here

How to Reach Nitara

Mukteshwar is 5–6 hours from Delhi by road. We also offer private transfer arrangements.

01

Delhi → Haldwani

Take NH 9 (Expressway) from Delhi. Haldwani is approximately 280 km from Delhi — about 5.5 hours by car. This is the main foothills gateway to the Kumaon region.

02

Haldwani → Bhimtal → Bhowali

From Haldwani, take the scenic mountain road via Bhimtal and Bhowali. This stretch is 38 km — approximately 1.5 hours with Himalayan views opening at every bend.

03

Bhowali → Mukteshwar → Dhanachuli

Continue 32 km from Bhowali to Mukteshwar via Ramgarh. Nitara Stays is located at Dhanachuli, 5 km beyond Mukteshwar on the Mukteshwar–Almora road.

✦ Private Transfer: We arrange private car transfers from Delhi, Kathgodam, and Pantnagar. Contact us to arrange pickup.
01

Fly to Pantnagar Airport (PGH)

The nearest airport is Pantnagar (PGH), 110 km from Mukteshwar. IndiGo and Air India operate daily flights from Delhi (45 min). Booking 2–3 weeks ahead is recommended.

02

Pantnagar to Mukteshwar

Pre-book a taxi from Pantnagar Airport directly to Dhanachuli — approximately 2.5–3 hours (110 km). We recommend Kumaon Cab Service (+91 94100 12345) or a Nitara transfer.

03

Nitara Private Transfer

Let us handle your arrival. Our private airport pickup service from Pantnagar — in a Toyota Innova — ensures a seamless, comfortable start to your mountain experience.

✦ Helicopter Option: Charter helicopter services from Delhi to Pantnagar are available for a truly grand arrival. Contact our concierge for partner details.
01

Train to Kathgodam

Kathgodam (KGM) is the last railhead — 95 km from Mukteshwar. Key trains: Ranikhet Express (14315 from Delhi), Shatabdi from Delhi (6 hours). Book sleeper 4AC for overnight travel.

02

Kathgodam → Mukteshwar

Taxis depart regularly from Kathgodam station to Mukteshwar and Dhanachuli — 3 hours (95 km) through the foothills. Share cabs are also available for ₹200–400/person.

03

KMOU Bus Option

Kumaon Motor Owners Union (KMOU) operates regular buses from Haldwani bus stand to Mukteshwar. Budget-friendly at ₹120–180. Journey time approximately 3.5 hours.

✦ Recommended: Ranikhet Express (14315) departs Old Delhi at 22:10, arrives Kathgodam at 05:25. Our driver can meet you at the station at dawn.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Mukteshwar is extraordinary year-round. March–June brings blooming rhododendrons, apple orchards in blossom, and warm days with clear Himalayan views. September–November offers crystal-clear skies and the best mountain panoramas — ideal for trekking. December–February delivers snowfall, crackling fires and unmatched romance. Monsoon (July–August) brings lush greenery but mist may obscure peak views.
